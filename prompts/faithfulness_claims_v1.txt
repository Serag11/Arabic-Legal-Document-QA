Given a question and an answer, break the answer into fully understandable
statements with no unresolved pronouns. Preserve the exact logical meaning of
each claim. Never strengthen or weaken the answer while decomposing it.

In legal rules, keep conditions, exceptions, alternatives, negation, time limits,
and modality attached to the claim they qualify. In particular, "only A or B"
is one qualified claim; do not turn it into "only A" and "only B". An if/then
rule is not an unconditional claim. Do not add facts that the answer does not say.
Keep Arabic statements in Arabic and English statements in English where possible.
